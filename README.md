## How my website came about – the process and working with AI

With this section I want to close my project properly, because in the end it turned out differently than I had planned after the intermediate presentation. But one thing at a time.

**Finding the idea**
After the intermediate presentation I kept working on my site locally, trying to find out what I could change and how to restructure it. My previous version still included a blog and separate project pages, and these were exactly the points that came up negatively in the presentation. So I kept experimenting, looked for more inspiration online and tried to find something that fit. Nothing really convinced me. Even when I started using Claude (although I originally wanted to leave AI out completely) I didn't find anything I was happy with. The ideas would have been doable with what we learned in the course, but I didn't want to build a boring CV HTML page that looks like any generic template.

About a week ago I finally had an idea I really wanted to follow and use for my final submission. The direction stayed the same (a personal website) but I suddenly thought of combining it with the theme of Apple's "Find My" app and projecting it onto my own life: where am I right now, either at university in Lüneburg or at work in Hamburg... 

**Working with AI**
I liked the idea so much that I first thought about whether it was even possible with what we had learned. It definitely was, but not without help. Since I wanted to realise the idea as well as possible, I decided to use AI after all. Together with Claude I worked out a concept, shared my design ideas and suggestions and made the decisions on design and content. So the idea was mine, and Claude helped me put it into practice.

**Making sure I learn something**
This course is called Web Programming and not Claude Prompting, so I didn't want to leave it at that. I asked myself whether my approach is justifiable, and for me the answer was yes... under one condition. I honestly did not practise writing code much in this final project. What I did instead was focus on realising an idea and getting the best out of it. Once the website was finished, I worked through all the code line by line. Most of it covers concepts from our course; the parts I didn't fully understand I had explained to me. I wrote my own comments into the code (where something stood out to me, or where I wrote an explanation down to remember it and repeat it by writing it myself). This took quite some effort, just like the design work and the content I wrote.

I worked on everything locally on my computer and didn't publish it until now, because I first wanted to go through the complete code. I tested the new site in a separate `redesign` branch in VS Code before merging it.

**My conclusion**
In my opinion AI helped me reach a better result... not as a shortcut where I handed over the learning process and the thinking, but as a way to realise my creativity and get the best possible result, while paying close attention to meeting the requirements.

I hope this transparent approach is understandable and of course I hope my website makes you smile (:   :)
